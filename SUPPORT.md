# Tethered Home support

Tethered Home helps households keep plans, shopping, care, car reminders, routines and wellbeing connected

## Getting started

Open the app, choose a household name, add household members in Settings and grant only the Apple permissions you want to use

## Household sharing

The household owner opens Settings, chooses Set up iCloud household sharing, then Invite household member

The invited person accepts the Apple invitation and opens Tethered Home with the same Apple ID

## Permissions and privacy

Calendar, Reminders, Health, Apple Music and notifications are optional

Permissions can be reviewed and revoked in Tethered Home Settings or Apple Settings

Wellbeing is personal by default and the on-device AI coach does not send prompts to a server

## Sync

For missing changes, open Settings and choose Sync household now

Confirm devices use the same Apple account or that the invited member accepted the household share

## Subscription

Apple shows the introductory offer and local renewal price

Use Restore purchases if access is missing
