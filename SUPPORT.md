# Tethered Home support

Tethered Home helps households keep plans, shopping, care, car reminders, routines and wellbeing connected

## Getting started

Open Tethered Home and complete the welcome steps

Choose a household name and add adults, children, pets and people you care for in Settings

Grant only the Apple features you want to use

## Household sharing

The household owner opens Settings, chooses Set up iCloud household sharing and then Invite household member

The invited person accepts the Apple invitation and opens Tethered Home using the same Apple ID that accepted it

Their profile is added to the household after the shared household is accepted

The household owner purchases Tethered Home and invites other members through the app’s iCloud household share. A member who accepts that invitation receives access through the household and does not need a separate subscription or Apple Family Sharing membership

## Permissions

Calendar and Reminders access lets Tethered Home read relevant plans and reminders and write events when you ask it to

Health access is optional and is used for selected wellness, hydration and activity records

Apple Music access is optional and is used for music rediscovery and playback controls

Notifications are optional and can be revoked in iPhone or iPad Settings

You can review and revoke access in Settings inside Tethered Home or in the Apple Settings app

## Sync

Tethered Home uses your private iCloud account and the shared household CloudKit area for household data

If a device is not showing recent changes, open Settings and choose Sync household now

Confirm both devices use the same Apple account or that the invited person has accepted the household share

## Wellbeing

Wellbeing is personal by default and is not sent as a family notification

Eligible Watch activities can record a HealthKit workout when permission is available

Completed wellbeing moments can contribute to personal points, family points, streaks and positive awards

The on-device AI coach is optional and does not send prompts to a server

## Subscription

The introductory offer and renewal price are shown by Apple and may use the currency of the Apple Media and Purchases storefront

Manage or cancel the subscription through Apple account subscription settings

Use Restore purchases if access is missing after reinstalling or changing devices

## Privacy and data controls

Tethered Home does not require a separate account for its local experience

You can leave a household or delete local Tethered Home data from Settings

CloudKit data is controlled by the iCloud account and household sharing state
